# Ginga Eiyuu Densetsu — English Translation

**Version 1.0.1** — [Download the BPS patch](ginga-eiyuu-densetsu-english-v1.0.1.bps)

Complete English translation of the Super Famicom game.
Narration and dialogue follow a space-opera style. Character, ship and place
names follow Gineipaedia; German names retain their spelling and diacritics.

## AI disclosure

**This translation was generated with AI using OpenAI Codex.**
The project owner's language, style and naming choices guided the work.
AI was also used for ROM
analysis, tooling, graphical lettering and automated emulator checks.
This is an unofficial fan translation.

## How to apply

Use a BPS-compatible patcher to apply the included .bps patch to an unmodified,
headerless Japanese ROM. Open the patched result in your emulator.
The original ROM is not included in this package.

## Verification

571 main entries in both text widths, 121 external name/rank/faction records
and 137 translated graphics resources were verified through the original
65816 engine. The checksum and exact BPS reconstruction were checked.
Cold-boot routes for all three scenarios passed in MesenCE 2.2.1 on Linux.
20 report/faction/window combinations were tested with the native loaders.

The project registry contains 167 names and terms with their sources.
Tassili transliterates the game's Japanese name; no matching article was
found in the selected wiki. The game's alternate branches are preserved,
including events that differ from the novels or OVA.

Naming reference: https://gineipaedia.com/
Tools: translate-retro-game, pinned project-local copy.
